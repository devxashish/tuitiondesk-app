How to Link WhatsApp to TuitionDesk

WhatsApp security requires linking directly inside the Android App

Method 1: Same Phone Link (Recommended)
1 Apne phone me TuitionDesk App open karein.
2 Top right me WhatsApp icon par tap karein.
3 Same Phone Link tab chunein aur "Tap Here to Enter Phone Number" dabayein.
4 Apna 10 digit WhatsApp number daalein ➔ Screen par 8-digit Pairing Code dikhega.
5 WhatsApp open karke ye 8-digit code daal dein ➔ App instantly background me link ho jayegi!
Method 2: Scan QR with Another Device

Agar aap kisi doosre phone ya laptop se scan karna chahte hain, to TuitionDesk app ke andar "Scan QR Code" tab par click karein. Wahan live, genuine WhatsApp Web QR load hoga jise WhatsApp camera turant scan kar lega.
